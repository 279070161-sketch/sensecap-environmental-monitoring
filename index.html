Real-world deployments

South Africa • Vineyards

Precision Weather Monitoring for High-Value Vineyards

SenseCAP S700-C collects localized data on solar radiation, radar rainfall, temperature, humidity, barometric pressure, and wind speed, helping grape growers in the Upington region optimize irrigation, prepare earlier for heat and flood risks, and protect grape yield and quality.

Portugal • Smart City

Urban Microclimate Monitoring for Smarter City Management

SenseCAP S600-A provides localized six-parameter weather data for heat-risk assessment, public-space management, and climate-responsive urban planning, giving Lisbon’s municipal weather monitoring authorities clearer environmental insights than regional forecasts alone.

Germany • Wind Energy

On-Site Weather Monitoring for Wind Farm Operations

SenseCAP S500 measures ultrasonic wind speed and direction, temperature, humidity, and barometric pressure. By combining site-level weather data with turbine telemetry, it supports data-informed operations at a wind farm near Germany’s North Sea coast, helping strengthen severe-weather early warning, improve power generation efficiency, and forecast power output.
